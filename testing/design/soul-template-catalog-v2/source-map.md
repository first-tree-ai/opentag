# Source-to-template design map

These inspected references come from Agency Agents and Awesome OpenClaw Agents. Every link is pinned to an observed
commit so the approved catalog's provenance can be checked without retaining the exploratory research history.

The role scope, names, icons, summaries, overviews, and bilingual bodies are OpenTag editorial decisions. The bodies are independently written, informed by the referenced working methods. A source match does not establish prompt effectiveness or demand for this catalog.

## Adaptation boundaries

- Specialist sources are not treated as complete definitions of broad Engineering, Design, Product, Research, or Writing roles.
- The proposal omits provider-specific tools and protocols, assumed collaborators, fixed schedules, invented histories, persistent-memory claims, fixed business results, universal experiment thresholds, and automatic outbound activity.
- Concrete methods are conditional on the task. A small request does not require a PRD, a complete design system, a research protocol, or a statistical test.
- The eight-template scope is a product proposal. It has not been validated by usage data or model benchmarks.
